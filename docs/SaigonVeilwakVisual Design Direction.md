## Primary Style: Immersive Editorial Travel UI

Use the following reference as the **primary visual and aesthetic inspiration**:

https://dribbble.com/shots/26856174-AI-Travel-Mobile-App-UI

Also use the attached travel application screenshots as visual references.

**Our goal is to create a premium, cinematic, immersive travel application — NOT a traditional fantasy RPG interface.**

Saigon: Veilwake is a location-based cultural tourism experience that combines real-world exploration, storytelling, collectible memories, and augmented reality.

### 1. Core Design Philosophy

The application should feel like a beautifully designed travel companion that gradually reveals a hidden magical world.

Prioritize:

- **Photography-first:** Real destinations, architecture, landscapes, and interactive maps are the visual center of the experience.
- **Immersive full-screen layouts:** Let the destination fill most of the screen, with minimal floating UI elements.
- **Editorial elegance:** Sophisticated typography, spacious composition, strong visual hierarchy, and carefully curated travel photography.
- **Minimal floating controls:** Rounded, translucent buttons and lightweight navigation overlays.
- **Contextual information:** Use elegant destination cards and draggable bottom sheets instead of permanently occupying the screen with large panels.
- **Cinematic atmosphere:** Natural lighting, beautiful color grading, subtle gradients, depth, and smooth transitions.
- **Premium travel aesthetic:** The application must feel polished, modern, calm, aspirational, and inviting.

The overall visual quality should resemble a premium travel discovery product, not a mobile RPG dashboard.

### 2. Color and Atmosphere

Do not make dark navy or black the dominant background color.

Use bright, natural, destination-inspired color palettes:

- Airy sky blue and soft white for daytime exploration.
- Warm sand, cream, and muted amber for historical places.
- Elegant deep blue and restrained violet for nighttime experiences.
- Turquoise and ocean blue for coastal exploration.

Allow the interface to adapt visually to its destination and time of day while preserving a consistent design system.

Use magical purple, luminous gold, and subtle particles only as carefully controlled accents for Veilwake's supernatural elements.

### 3. Mobile UI Design Language

Follow the visual language of the reference:

- Immersive full-bleed destination photography and maps.
- Elegant text over imagery with carefully controlled contrast.
- Soft translucent glass overlays.
- Rounded floating icon buttons.
- Minimal, refined destination information cards.
- Layered photography and destination thumbnails.
- Context-aware bottom sheets.
- Beautiful transitions between destinations and experiences.
- Clean, modern iconography.
- Subtle shadows, blur, and carefully considered spacing.

Avoid overly decorative fantasy borders, complex HUDs, excessive neon, aggressive gradients, or dense game-like interface elements.

### 4. Apply This Style to Veilwake's Existing Features

Preserve the established product architecture:

**Explore:** A large immersive interactive city map featuring real destinations, elegant location markers, nearby activities, and contextual discovery cards.

**Ký Môn:** The signature circular navigation control at the bottom center. It opens four sections: Explore, Journeys, Journal, and Passport. Keep this distinctive mechanism instead of replacing it with a conventional fixed four-tab navigation bar.

**NPC Companion:** A discreet floating assistant on the bottom left, integrated naturally into the travel interface.

**AR Lens:** A contextual camera entry on the bottom right.

**Journeys:** Beautiful travel itineraries presented as editorial destination cards and immersive stories.

**Journal:** A refined story and evidence journal, inspired by travel notebooks rather than an RPG quest log.

**Passport:** A personal collection of travel memories, customizable location badges, photographs, and meaningful cultural discoveries. Draw inspiration from beautiful travel postcards and collectible stamps.

**AR Experiences:** Keep the normal travel interface elegant and realistic. Introduce more expressive magical visuals only when activating a special AR encounter or entering the hidden world.

### 5. Design Priorities

1. Premium travel experience.
2. Immersive real-world exploration.
3. Beautiful destination photography and maps.
4. Minimal, intuitive mobile interaction.
5. Elegant storytelling.
6. Subtle gamification.
7. Selective magical AR effects.

**Tourism comes first. Gamification and fantasy enhance the experience rather than dominate it.**

### 6. What to Avoid

- Traditional mobile RPG interfaces.
- Dark fantasy dashboards across the entire application.
- Excessive glowing borders and magical icons.
- Oversized quest panels, XP bars, or inventory-style layouts.
- Cluttered navigation and excessive buttons.
- Generic SaaS dashboards.
- Cartoonish or childish visual design.
- Copying the Egypt content, branding, or exact composition from the reference.

### 7. Final Design Requirement

Maintain a consistent, recognizable **Immersive Editorial Travel UI** identity across every screen.

The application should make users think:

**"I want to explore this destination."**

Before making them think:

**"I want to complete this game quest."**

Use the reference for its visual principles and interaction quality, while creating an original design identity for Saigon: Veilwake.

Preserve the existing Veilwake gameplay mechanics and information architecture. Refine the visual presentation, not the established product behavior.
